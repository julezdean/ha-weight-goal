weight-goal-card render harness

The built bundle from custom_components/weight_goal/www/weight-goal-card.js, rendered against a fake Home Assistant. Buttons, the weight field and the goal editor really write: they change the fake state and every card showing that goal redraws, the same way the real instance behaves.

Faked: ha-card and ha-icon, the hass object, and the service layer. Icons are placeholder glyphs at the exact size a real icon occupies — the name is on data-icon. The card editor is not covered, it needs ha-form.
